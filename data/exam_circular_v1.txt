=========================================
COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
ACADEMIC OFFICE - OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR
CIRCULAR: END-SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS (SPRING 2026)
=========================================
Circular No: CET/REG/EXAM/2026-V1
Date: March 10, 2026

To: All Students and Faculty Members

Subject: Schedule and Rules for End-Semester Examinations - May 2026

1. EXAMINATION DATES
   - The Spring 2026 End-Semester Examinations will commence on May 11, 2026, and conclude on May 29, 2026.
   - Lab/Practical examinations will be conducted between May 4, 2026, and May 8, 2026.
   - Detailed branch-wise timetables will be published on the College Academic Portal by April 15, 2026.

2. ADMIT CARD GENERATION
   - Admit cards will be available for download from May 1, 2026.
   - Note: Students with less than 75% attendance or outstanding fee dues will not be permitted to download their admit cards.

3. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
   - A minimum of 75% cumulative attendance in each course is mandatory to sit for the final examination.
   - Medical waivers up to 10% (minimum 65% attendance) may be granted by the Dean of Academic Affairs upon submission of valid medical documents to the department office by April 20, 2026.

4. EXAM DAY INSTRUCTIONS & RULES
   - Reporting Time: Students must arrive at the designated exam hall 30 minutes before the scheduled exam start.
   - Entry Requirements: Physical copy of College ID Card and printed Admit Card are mandatory for entry.
   - Prohibited Items: Smartwatches, mobile phones, calculators with programming features, bags, and loose notes/paper are strictly prohibited.
   - Plagiarism/Cheating: Any student found cheating or possessing unauthorized material will be referred to the Disciplinary Committee, resulting in an automatic 'F' grade in the course and potential suspension.

5. ARREAR / MAKEUP EXAMINATIONS
   - Arrear examinations for previous semesters will run concurrently with the regular schedule.
   - Makeup exams for students who missed exams due to medical emergencies (with approved medical leaves) will be conducted in the first week of July 2026.
