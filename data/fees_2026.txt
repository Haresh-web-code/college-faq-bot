=========================================
COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
OFFICIAL FEE STRUCTURE - ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027
=========================================

1. TUITION FEES (Per Semester)
   - Undergraduate (B.Tech / B.E.): $4,500
   - Postgraduate (M.Tech / M.E.): $5,500
   - Doctoral (Ph.D.): $3,000

2. HOSTEL & MESS CHARGES (Per Semester)
   - Single Room (AC): $1,200
   - Double Sharing (AC): $900
   - Double Sharing (Non-AC): $600
   - Mess Fee (Standard Meal Plan): $500 (Mandatory for hostellers)

3. LABORATORY & LIBRARY DEPOSIT (One-time, Refundable)
   - Security Deposit: $300 (Payable at the time of admission)

4. IMPORTANT DEADLINES & LATE FEE POLICY
   - Odd Semester Fee Payment Deadline: August 15, 2026
   - Even Semester Fee Payment Deadline: January 15, 2027
   - Late Fee Fine:
     * Up to 7 days after deadline: $50
     * 8 to 15 days after deadline: $150
     * Beyond 15 days: Suspension of library and academic portal access.

5. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID
   - Merit-based Scholarship: 50% tuition waiver for students with GPA > 3.8/4.0.
   - Need-based Aid: Up to 30% fee reduction based on household annual income (limit under $30,000 per year).
   - Scholarship Application Deadline: July 30, 2026. Contact the Financial Aid Office (Office 102) for details.
